Candela Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Candela (“the app”) is a native macOS menu-bar utility that controls displays attached to your Mac. This policy describes what the Mac App Store version does and does not collect.

Who we are

Candela is published by Yun Wu. Contact: wuyun768@icloud.com. Source code: github.com/WymanY/Candela.

Data the app does not collect

Candela does not create an account, does not require a login, and does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not upload personal data to us or to any server we operate.

Data stored on your Mac

Preferences stay on the device in standard macOS storage, including:

  • display names, last brightness, volume, mute, contrast, input, rotation, and Picture in Picture layout
  • saved scenes and the remembered speaker output for those scenes
  • settings such as launch at login, software dimming, and restore-on-reconnect

Those values never leave the Mac unless you copy them yourself.

Permissions

  • Screen Recording is used only to capture a display or window for Picture in Picture, the monitor wall, and software volume on HDMI/DisplayPort speakers that have no hardware slider. Frames stay in memory on your Mac and are not saved or uploaded.
  • Microphone / audio capture is used only so software volume can attenuate digital monitor speakers. Audio is processed locally and is not recorded or transmitted.

Candela does not need Accessibility, Full Disk Access, or Contacts. It does not browse the web or open unrestricted internet content.

Local control socket

While Candela is running, the optional CLI and MCP helper can talk to a local Unix socket at ~/Library/Application Support/Candela/control.sock. That socket is only on your Mac and is not exposed to the network.

Children

Candela is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions: wuyun768@icloud.com or GitHub issues.

Candela 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 8 月 17 日

Candela（“本应用”）是一款原生 macOS 菜单栏工具，用于控制连接到 Mac 的显示器。本政策说明 Mac App Store 版本会如何处理数据。

开发者

发布者：Yun Wu。联系邮箱：wuyun768@icloud.com。源代码：github.com/WymanY/Candela。

我们不会收集的数据

Candela 不提供账户、不要求登录，也不包含分析、广告、崩溃上报或其他第三方追踪 SDK。应用不会把个人数据上传给我们，也不会上传到由我们运营的服务器。

仅保存在本机的数据

偏好设置只写在本机的标准 macOS 存储中，包括：

  • 显示器名称，以及上次的亮度、音量、静音、对比度、输入、旋转和画中画布局
  • 已保存的场景，以及该场景记住的扬声器输出
  • 登录时启动、软件调光、重新接入时恢复等开关

除非你自己拷贝，这些数据不会离开这台 Mac。

权限

  • 屏幕录制仅用于画中画、监视墙，以及在没有硬件滑条的 HDMI/DisplayPort 音箱上做软件音量。画面只在本机内存中处理，不会保存或上传。
  • 麦克风 / 音频采集仅用于给数字显示器音箱做软件衰减。音频只在本地处理，不会录音或外传。

Candela 不需要辅助功能、完全磁盘访问或通讯录。它也不会浏览网页或打开不受限的互联网内容。

本机控制接口

Candela 运行时，可选的 CLI 和 MCP 助手可以通过本机 Unix 套接字 ~/Library/Application Support/Candela/control.sock 通信。该套接字只存在于本机，不会对网络开放。

儿童

Candela 是通用工具，不以儿童为对象，也不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

变更

如本政策有更新，新文本会发布在本页面，并更新“最近更新”日期。

联系

如有问题，请发邮件至 wuyun768@icloud.com，或在 GitHub issues 提问。
